BillSplit · for iPhone

Split the bill.
Keep it fair.

Split any bill item by item. Assign each item, share tax and tip proportionally, and see what everyone owes. It saves profiles, so similar bills are matched automatically and split the way you chose. It uses Apple Intelligence with Private Cloud Compute, which keeps no data. Free to use, secure and private. BillSplit does not transfer bill payments or send payment requests. Optional support tips use Apple’s in-app purchase system.
