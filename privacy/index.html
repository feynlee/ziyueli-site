Visiting this site

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. To send you the page, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details, as any web host does. I do not receive these logs. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to them. The fonts are hosted on this site, and there are no ads or third-party trackers.

Analytics (only if you accept)

I use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (country and city), and which device and browser they use. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID. These cookies are set only after you press Accept in the cookie box. If you press Decline, or ignore the box, nothing is loaded and no cookie is set.

Google signals and ad personalization are switched off. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. Event data is kept for 2 months. The legal basis is your consent, and you can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Booking a call

The "Book a 30-minute call" button opens a Google Calendar booking page. You enter your first name, last name and email address. Google processes this data to run the booking page, and I receive it as a calendar event with a Google Meet link. I use it only to arrange the call, hold it and follow up on it. I keep it for as long as needed for the call and for any work that follows, and then delete it. You can ask me to delete it sooner. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before an agreement, and my interest in answering enquiries.

Email

If you email me, I read your message and reply. I keep the emails as long as they are needed for the conversation or the work, and then delete them.

Who receives data
